Assumptions

EXAMPLE assumptions, replace with your own program rules. Profit: 15% of new business, 7% of other business. Monthly bonus: $250 at base ($5,000 each month), +$25 per $500 above. Next-month bonus: $1,000 when the prior month's new business is $5,000+. Car bonus $400/month. Recruiting bonus: $25 per recruit. Campaign bonus: $5,000 at $200K total + $50K new business. Year-end performance and growth bonuses are not in campaign net. To change these amounts, edit the constants at the top of the script in this page.
