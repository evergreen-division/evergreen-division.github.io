Why the gift matters

Clients retell the whole experience through a few moments, and the handoff is one of them. Wine gets finished and gift cards get spent. A gift used every day keeps your name in the conversation for years.

How the math works

Revenue left on the table = clients per year × extra referral rate × profit per client. Net gain subtracts what you spend on gifts. Each referral is counted once, with no compounding.

What the consult covers

Your closing volume, what you give today, and one upcoming client to start with. You leave with a gift plan sized to your business, whether or not we work together.
