Conversion rates: division actuals

Conversion rates are example division actuals: 3 people set for interview gets 1 recruit, and 3 recruits get 1 launch. So each launch costs 9 sets. 40 × 9 = 360. Spread across the 9 weeks that averages 40 per week. If top-50 recruits convert better than 3:1, the real number will be lower, but plan on your own actuals. Replace the rates with yours.

Method: division best practice

From the division meeting: top-50 lists are the goose that lays the golden egg, and they stay manager-owned. Set your internal deadline two weeks before the official one to chase stragglers. Brainstorm prompt if someone stalls: open Snapchat. You have hundreds of contacts, we only need 50. Ask for the early mover by name today, not from the podium.

Evergreen Division · Winter Break Recruiting Plan

If a manager is away during a launch window

When a manager is remote, texting, interviews, ad monitoring and Zoom meetings all continue. What cannot be done from abroad is anything physical (posters, tables, in-person training), so assistant managers own the campuses and cover the trainings that fall in those windows. AMs may not be home for break, so their lane is entirely phone and social.

Sets total 360 · peak 65 in Dec wk2

The Sets column is how many interviews you need booked that week, totaling 360. It is front-loaded so that, at every launch, enough sets were already booked in earlier weeks: by each launch week, cumulative sets run ahead of the 9-per-launch requirement. Peak weeks (Dec wk1 and wk2, 60–65 sets) are double the 30-fire standard. That is where every manager and AM needs booking blocks on the calendar.
